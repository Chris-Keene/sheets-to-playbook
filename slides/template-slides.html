How it gets made

From your sheets to the playbook

  1. Talk and write

    Your table fills in a sheet for each question. A side sheet catches quotes, questions and resources.

  2. We photograph the sheets

    Sheets only, never people. Nothing is recorded.

  3. AI reads, people check

    AI reads every sheet. People check each line against the photos before anything goes live.

  4. The playbook goes live

    Soon after today. The QR code on your commitment card takes you there.We’ll tell you where to find it.

Chatham House Rule. Nothing said today is attributed to anyone. A name only appears in the playbook on a commitment card, and only if you write it there.
