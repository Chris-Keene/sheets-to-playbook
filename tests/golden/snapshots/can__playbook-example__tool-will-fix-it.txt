BELIEF A TOOL OR AI WILL FIX IT

When leaders think a tool or AI has solved it
Worked on at table 11
Technology
Financial services

When a new tool or AI assistant arrives, leaders can assume accessibility is now handled. Manual testing and testing with disabled people are the first things cut.

We bought a scanner and the next question was whether we still needed the accessibility team.

AI writes code fast, including inaccessible code.

What’s been tried
Helped
Explaining what automated tools can and can't find, with a short list of examples.
A budget line for testing with disabled people, kept separate from tooling.
Got in the way
Reporting the tool's score as the only measure. It became the target.

What to do
Write down what your tools check, and what still needs people.
What you need

How it sticks

What to do
Ring-fence time and money for testing with disabled people.
What you need

How it sticks
Report user-testing findings next to the tool results each quarter.
