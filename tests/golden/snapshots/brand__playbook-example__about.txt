ABOUT THIS PLAYBOOK

How this playbook was made

This playbook was written by the people in the room at the team workshop at Northfield Accessibility Summit 2026 on Thursday 4 June 2026, 11:30 to 12:30. People came from different organisations, sectors and stages: some just starting out, some working alone, some running established programmes.

From the tables to this page
Each of the 12 tables worked through four questions on large printed sheets: where support gets stuck, what has helped or not, what to try next, and one personal commitment.
A separate sheet on every table collected good quotes, open questions and resources as they came up.
After the session, the Acme team photographed every sheet.
Claude, an AI model, transcribed the sheets and grouped the challenges into themes. It was told not to add anything that wasn’t written down, and to flag anything it couldn’t read.
The Acme team checked every entry against the original sheets before publishing.
Who said what

The session ran under the Chatham House Rule. Nothing from the table discussions is linked to a named person or organisation. Commitments show a name only where someone chose to add one.

Keep a copy

This playbook is a single web page that works offline, so you can keep it, share it or open it without an internet connection.
