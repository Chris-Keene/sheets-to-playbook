HOW IT WAS MADE

How these notes were made

Each team wrote on three paper sheets during the session. Afterwards the sheets were photographed, the handwriting was read with the help of AI, and a person checked every uncertain word against the photos.

Three questions
How did it go? What went well, and what got in the way.
What could we try? A “How might we…” question, ideas, and three dot votes each.
What we’ll do. Actions, each with who and by when.
Nothing added

Every line here traces back to a sheet: nothing was added, and quotes and questions are word for word. Vote counts are the dots on the paper. If a word couldn’t be read for certain, a person checked it against the photo.
