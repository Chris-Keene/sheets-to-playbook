How it works
 From your sheets to the Workshop Notes
 Talk and writeEach table fills in a sheet for each question. Anyone can speak; one person writes.
 We photograph the sheetsSheets only, never people. Nothing is recorded.
 AI reads, people checkAI reads every sheet. People check each line against the photos before anything goes out.
 The Workshop Notes arrivesSoon after today. We’ll send it round.
 The last of the welcome: Question 1 starts at minute 5.
 Thick black markers only, so the photos are readable.
