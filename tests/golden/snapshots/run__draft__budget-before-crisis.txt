MONEY ONLY COMES AFTER COMPLAINTS

Getting budget before something goes wrong
Worked on at tables 5 and 9
Local government
Higher education
Charity
Utilities
Telecoms

Money tends to arrive only after complaints, and the budget often sits with IT rather than product. Waiting for legal to push it, or asking for a big programme all at once, hasn't worked.

Small and recurring beats big and one-off
