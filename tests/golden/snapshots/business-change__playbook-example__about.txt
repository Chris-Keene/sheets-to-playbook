HOW IT WAS MADE

How these notes were made

Each table wrote on 5 paper sheets during the session. Afterwards the sheets were photographed, the handwriting was read with the help of AI, and a person checked every uncertain word against the photos.

5 questions
Who does this change affect? Change.
Who needs to be involved? Person.
How ready are we? Area.
What could go wrong? Risk.
What we'll do Action.
Nothing added

Every line here traces back to a sheet: nothing was added, and anything quoted is word for word. Numbers, like votes or scores, are what was written on the paper. If a word couldn’t be read for certain, a person checked it against the photo.
