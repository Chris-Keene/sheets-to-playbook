Team workshop
 Welcome to the session. Great to have you here!
 Minutes 0 to 10: the welcome, from this slide up to Question 1.
 The hour at a glance, in minutes from the start:
 0 to 10: welcome
 10 to 22: Question 1 (7 minutes on the sheet, then 5 with the table cards, on one 12-minute timer)
 22 to 35: Question 2 (13 minutes)
 35 to 50: Question 3 (15 minutes)
 50 to 55: Question 4, the commitment card (5 minutes)
 55 to 60: share-back and close (skip the share-back if you’re running late)

--- slide 3
How it gets made
 From your sheets to the playbook
 Talk and writeYour table fills in a sheet for each question. A side sheet catches quotes, questions and resources.
 We photograph the sheetsSheets only, never people. Nothing is recorded.
 AI reads, people checkAI reads every sheet. People check each line against the photos before anything goes live.
 The playbook goes liveSoon after today. The QR code on your commitment card takes you there.
 Chatham House Rule. Nothing said today is attributed to anyone. A name only appears in the playbook on a commitment card, and only if you write it there.
 The last of the welcome: Question 1 starts at minute 10.
 Say: there are no wrong answers, and nobody has to write. Anyone can speak and the table lead or side-sheet keeper writes it down.
 Thick black markers only, so the photos are readable.
