NO BUDGET OR HEADCOUNT

Securing budget before there's a crisis
Worked on at tables 5 and 12
Retail
Local government

Without a line in the budget, accessibility runs on goodwill and spare time. Money tends to appear after a complaint or a legal letter, then disappears once the fix is in.

We got budget the week after a legal letter. We lost it the year after we fixed it.

Ask for a small amount that's guaranteed rather than a big amount that's one-off.

What’s been tried
Helped
Bidding for a small recurring amount inside an existing budget, such as the design system or QA.
Costing what late fixes cost, using real examples from the past year.
Sharing training costs with HR and the disability staff network.
Got in the way
One big business case for a full programme. It was too big to approve.
Funding tied to one project, which ended when the project did.

What to do
Find two or three real issues that were fixed late and estimate what they cost compared with fixing them in design.
What you need
Time from a delivery lead to estimate the effort.
How it sticks
Refresh the examples each year so the case stays current.

What you need

How it sticks
