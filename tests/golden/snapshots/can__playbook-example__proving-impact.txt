HARD TO PROVE IMPACT

Proving impact in terms leaders care about
Worked on at tables 3 and 9
Insurance
Higher education
Technology

Leaders ask for numbers, but the changes that matter most, like fewer barriers for customers and more confident teams, are hard to count. Issue counts can even go up when things are improving, because more people are finally looking.

Our issue count doubled and leadership panicked. It doubled because we'd finally started looking.

One video of a customer using a screen reader on our app did more than a year of dashboards.

What’s been tried
Helped
A before-and-after of one real customer journey instead of a score for the whole estate.
Tracking issues found before release against issues found by customers.
Linking complaints and contact-centre calls to specific barriers.
Got in the way
A single compliance percentage. It hid the important barriers and people learned to game it.
Reporting everything. Nobody read a 40-page audit summary.
What we learned
Pick measures that show where problems are caught, not just how many there are.

Measure where issues are found
What to do
Report the share of issues caught before release against those found after.
What you need
A tag in your issue tracker for where each issue was found.
How it sticks
Add it to the team's existing quality dashboard instead of a separate report.

Work with customer support to tag contacts that mention accessibility.

How it sticks
Send support a quarterly summary of what changed because of their tags.
