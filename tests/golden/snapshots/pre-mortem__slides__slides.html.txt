 Question 1It’s six months from now and the project has failed. One reason per row: what went wrong, what we’d do about it, how likely it is.

 Minutes 5 to 25. Read the question out in full. 20 minutes.
 Say it as if it has already happened: “the launch slipped because…”. It gets more honest answers than “what might go wrong?”.Write first, talk second: two minutes of quiet writing before anyone shares.
