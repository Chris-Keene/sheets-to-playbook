RUN YOUR OWN

Run your own forum

Everything used to run this session: the plan for the hour, what’s on each sheet, prompts for when a table gets stuck, and the print files. Take it, change it, make it yours.

Free to use and adapt under CC BY 4.0: please credit the sheets-to-playbook kit by Chris Keene. Check the licence wording

The hour, in seven parts
Set-up and arrivals

Before

Put a kit on every table: the four sheets, a commitment card per person, a table card, thick black markers and sticky notes. Test the slides and clicker. Table leads write their table number on every sheet.

Welcome

0 to 10 minutes · 10 minutes

Who you are, and the playbook you’ll make from the tables. How it gets made: sheets, photos, people check every line. Chatham House Rule, and nobody has to write.

Question 1: Where does support get stuck?

10 to 22 minutes · 12 minutes

7 minutes to list every challenge and star the top three, then pick one to tackle. 5 minutes with the table card up: tables share what they’re tackling, and anyone can move tables.

Question 2: What has helped, or not?

22 to 35 minutes · 13 minutes

What helped, what got in the way, and what you’d do next time, about the challenge on the table card.

Question 3: What could people try now, and how does it stick?

35 to 50 minutes · 15 minutes

Up to three actions: what to try, what you need, how it sticks. Tick who each one is good for.

Question 4: One thing I will try, adapt or explore

50 to 55 minutes · 5 minutes

Everyone writes a commitment on both halves of their card, tears it, keeps the half with the QR code and leaves the other half on the table.

Share-back and close

55 to 60 minutes · 5 minutes

Tables share an action each, as time allows. Skip it if you’re running late.

Capture

After · About 30 minutes

Photograph every sheet with the table number in shot (sheets only, never people), and collect the card halves.

The slides

The deck used on the day, with a slide and a timer for each question and the facilitator’s notes and prompts. Change anything you like.

The slides (web page, N KB)
Opens in any browser and works offline. Arrow keys move, T starts each question’s timer, N shows the facilitator notes.
What’s on each sheet

The sheets are A1 posters, one per question, plus a side sheet that stays out all session. Here’s every word on them, to copy into your own versions.

SHEET 1 · QUESTION 1

Where does support get stuck?
Who’s at this table?
Everyone puts one tally mark against their sector, and writes their country. Sectors: Public sector, Finance, Retail, Tech, Education, Charity, Health, Other. Then a line for Countries.
Every challenge at this table
Write them all down. Budget, leadership, burnout, proving impact…
Our top three
Three numbered lines.
The one we’re tackling
Copy it onto your table card too.

SHEET 2 · QUESTION 2

What has helped, or not?
Helped
What happened, and why did it work?
Got in the way
What would you change or avoid?
If we did it again, we’d…

SHEET 3 · QUESTION 3

What could people try now, and how does it stick?
Action 1, Action 2 and Action 3
Each has three columns, What to try, What you need and How it sticks, and tick boxes: Good for Just starting, Growing, Established.

SIDE SHEET · ALL SESSION

Heard something good? Write it here.
Quotes
Exact words, no names
Questions
Things nobody could answer
Resources
Links, books, people, tools
Best thing said today
The table lead writes it in the last two minutes.

COMMITMENT CARD · QUESTION 4

One thing I will try, adapt or explore is…
Both halves
The same prompt and writing lines on each half. People write their commitment on both, tear along the perforation and keep one.
The half you keep
A QR code and “Scan for the Workshop Notes: what every table came up with.”
The half you leave
Name (optional) and Organisation (optional): “Add these only if you’re happy for them to appear in the public playbook.”

TABLE CARD

Folds into a tent
One side
The table number.
The other side
“We’re tackling:” with lines to write the table’s challenge, so the room can see it.

Every sheet’s footer says: “All of this goes into the Workshop Notes. No names, please.”

If a table gets stuck
Question 1
What would you fix first if you could?
What makes support drop off after a good start?
What does your boss ask that you can’t answer?
Question 2
Tell me about a time it worked. What was different?
What would you never try again?
What made the difference?
Question 3
What could someone do on Monday?
What would they need to make it happen?
What stops it fading after a month?
Question 4
Make it small enough to start this month. What’s one thing you heard today that you could try?
