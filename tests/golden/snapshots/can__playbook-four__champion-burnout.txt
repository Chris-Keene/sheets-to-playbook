CHAMPIONS BURNING OUT

Stopping champions burning out
Worked on at table 3
Media
Charity
Telecoms

Champions usually do this on top of their day job. The most committed people take on the most, nobody sees the extra hours, and they quietly step back.

Being the accessibility person at our place meant being the accessibility person for everything.

Our best champion left and said it felt like unpaid overtime with a badge.

What’s been tried
Helped
Protected time agreed with line managers, even two hours a month, written into objectives.
Rotating roles, so different champions host, answer questions and run training each quarter.
A clear list of what champions don't do, so they could say no to full audits.
Got in the way
Recognition that was only a certificate. People wanted it to count in their review.
An open chat channel where every question went to the same three people.

Showing all 3 actions.

Write champion work into objectives
What to do
Agree with line managers that champion work counts in performance reviews.
What you need
A short template objective, and HR or a senior sponsor to back it.
How it sticks
Make it part of joining the network, so every new champion starts with it agreed.
Good for
Growing
Established
Publish a 'champions don't' list
What to do
Set out what champions do and don't do, such as no full audits and no sign-off.
What you need
An hour with your core group to agree it, and somewhere visible to publish it.
How it sticks
Point to it every time a request goes over the line.
Good for
Just starting
Growing
Rotate the jobs
What to do
Split the network's work into roles and rotate them every quarter.
What you need
At least six active champions and a simple rota.
How it sticks
Hand over in a 15-minute call with a checklist.
