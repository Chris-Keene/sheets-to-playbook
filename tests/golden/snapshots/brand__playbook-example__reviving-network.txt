NETWORKS LOSE MOMENTUM

Reviving a champions network that has stalled
Worked on at table 2
Professional services

Networks often start with energy and a launch event, then attendance drops. A year later there are 200 names on the list and five people turning up.

We had 200 members and no idea which ones were actually champions.

What’s been tried
Helped
Asking everyone to opt back in. Fewer members, but the ones who stayed were active.
Giving each meeting one practical thing to take away instead of only updates.
Got in the way
Monthly meetings in the same format for two years.

Showing all 2 actions.

Run an opt-in reset
What to do
Ask every member to confirm they want to stay and what they want from the network.
What you need
A short form and a message from your sponsor.
How it sticks
Repeat it yearly so membership reflects who's active.

What you need
