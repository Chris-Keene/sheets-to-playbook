 Question 1Fill both columns: one thing per line. Short is fine.
 How did it go?What could we try?What we'll do
 Minutes 5 to 20. Read the question out in full. 15 minutes.
 Ask everyone to write before anyone talks: quiet writing first gets more voices onto the sheet.If a column stays empty, ask: what would you want to keep doing? What would you rather never do again?

Question 2·, Sheet 2
 15:00Start timer
 What could we try?
 How might we…Pick one thing that got in the way, and turn it into a question.IdeasOne idea per box. Then everyone puts three dots on the ideas they'd back.
 Question 2Turn one thing that got in the way into a question, then fill the boxes with ideas. Last two minutes: three dots each.
 How did it go?What could we try?What we'll do
 Minutes 20 to 35. Read the question out in full. 15 minutes.
 The question works best when it's specific: “How might we stop releases slipping in the last week?”, not “How might we be better?”Call time for dots with two minutes to go. Dots go on ideas, not on people.
